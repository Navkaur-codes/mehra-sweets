SESSION 10 STARTER PACK: BUILD A CHATBOT FOR A BUSINESS

1. Open Claude Code in this folder.
2. Find your business card in cards/.
3. Use the prompts in prompts.md, in order. Change only the parts in [square brackets].
4. Claude Code creates your bot in a new folder, my-bot/.

FILES
cards/           the three business cards
tests/           the 15 tests for each card (used by Prompt 4)
prompts.md       today's prompts (also in your handout)
worker.js        the bot your new one is built from
chat-widget.js   the chat bubble for your website
CLAUDE.md        instructions Claude Code reads automatically
extras/          optional: command-line deploy, Vercel version, Google Sheet

Never put your key in a file, on GitHub or in Claude.
