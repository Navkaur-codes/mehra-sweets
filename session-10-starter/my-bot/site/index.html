Gift boxes

For weddings, parties, festivals and office gifting.

How many boxes?

Orders and delivery

Our team replies to order enquiries on WhatsApp within 2 hours during opening hours.

Same-day pickup

Orders up to 5 kg can be picked up from the shop the same day.

Bulk orders

More than 5 kg, or 20 or more gift boxes: at least 2 days' notice. During Diwali week, 5 days' notice.

Home delivery

Within Ludhiana city on orders above Rs 1,500. Delivery charge Rs 100; free above Rs 5,000. No delivery outside Ludhiana and no courier for sweets.

Payment

Cash, UPI or card at the shop. For bulk orders, 50% advance by UPI to confirm.

Allergies: many of our sweets are made in the same kitchen, so we cannot promise that any sweet is free from nuts, gluten or dairy. Please speak to the shop about allergies.
