# Business card: Mehra Sweets

*A fictional business for a classroom exercise. All contact details are placeholders.*

## About
- Mehra Sweets is a sweet shop and namkeen maker in Ludhiana, Punjab, started in 1987.
- Sells from its shop counter and takes orders for festivals, weddings, parties and office gifting.
- Owner: Mrs. Anjali Mehra. During Diwali and the wedding season the phone never stops, and many orders are lost.

## Location and hours
- Shop: SCO 12, Main Market, Model Town, Ludhiana, Punjab.
- Open every day, 8:00 am to 10:00 pm, including Sundays. On Holi the shop opens at 4:00 pm.

## Contact
- Phone and WhatsApp: +91 98765 43210
- Email: orders@mehrasweets.example

## Price list (per kg, shop prices)
- Kaju katli: Rs 1,100
- Motichoor laddoo: Rs 560
- Pinni (desi ghee): Rs 640
- Gulab jamun: Rs 400
- Rasgulla: Rs 400
- Barfi (milk): Rs 600
- Mixed namkeen: Rs 360
- Gift boxes: 500 g box Rs 50 extra for packing; 1 kg box Rs 80 extra. Custom printed boxes for weddings and companies from 50 boxes (price on request).
- Prices can change during festivals. The team confirms the final price with every order.
- Planning tip the shop gives customers: for weddings and parties, most customers give one 500 g box per guest; for office gifting, one 1 kg box per person.
- The team replies to order enquiries on WhatsApp within 2 hours during opening hours.

## Orders and delivery
- Orders up to 5 kg can be picked up from the shop the same day.
- Bulk orders (more than 5 kg, or 20 or more gift boxes) need at least 2 days' notice. During Diwali week, 5 days' notice.
- Home delivery within Ludhiana city on orders above Rs 1,500. Delivery charge Rs 100; free above Rs 5,000.
- No delivery outside Ludhiana. No courier for sweets.
- Payment: cash, UPI or card at the shop. For bulk orders, 50% advance by UPI to confirm.

## What the owner wants the bot to do
1. Answer questions about sweets, prices, opening hours, location and delivery.
2. Help customers plan quantities for events (for example, how many boxes for 100 guests).
3. Take an order enquiry: name, WhatsApp number, delivery address or pickup, sweets with quantities, box type, and the date and time needed.
4. Treat orders above Rs 20,000, company gifting and orders needed within 2 days as high priority.

## Things the bot must never do
- Promise that any sweet is free from nuts, gluten or dairy. Many sweets are made in the same kitchen. Always tell the customer to speak to the shop about allergies.
- Confirm an order or a delivery time. Only the shop team confirms orders.
- Offer discounts.
- Promise delivery outside Ludhiana.
