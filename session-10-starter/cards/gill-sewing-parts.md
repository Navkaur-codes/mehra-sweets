# Business card: Gill Sewing Machine Parts

*A fictional business for a classroom exercise. All contact details are placeholders.*

## About
- Gill Sewing Machine Parts sells spare parts for domestic and industrial sewing machines, and repairs machines, in Ludhiana, Punjab. Started in 2003.
- Customers are tailors, garment units, and sewing machine dealers in other cities.
- Owner: Mr. Harpreet Gill. Most enquiries come on WhatsApp, and customers often don't know the part name, only the machine model.

## Location and hours
- Shop and workshop: Shop 7, Sewing Machine Market, near Clock Tower, Ludhiana, Punjab.
- Open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 7:00 pm. Sunday 10:00 am to 2:00 pm (counter sales only, no repairs).

## Contact
- Phone and WhatsApp: +91 98765 43210
- Email: parts@gillsewing.example

## What they sell
- Domestic machine parts: bobbins, bobbin cases, needles, presser feet, belts, motors, shuttle hooks and tension assemblies for common hand and electric machines.
- Industrial machine parts: needles, hooks, feed dogs, needle plates, presser feet, bobbins and motors for single-needle lockstitch, overlock and flatlock machines.
- Servo motors for industrial machines (energy-saving), 550 W and 750 W.
- Accessories: sewing oil, scissors, measuring tapes, machine covers.

## Repairs
- Repair and servicing of domestic and industrial machines at the workshop.
- Servicing for garment units at their premises in Ludhiana, for 5 or more machines at a time.
- Repair cost depends on the fault. A technician checks the machine first. The bot must not quote repair costs.

## How buying works
- Counter sales to anyone. Dealers in other cities can order in bulk.
- Dealer minimum order: Rs 10,000 per order.
- Parts are sent anywhere in India by courier or transport. The buyer pays freight.
- Prices change often, so the team shares the current price on WhatsApp. The bot must not quote prices.
- To find the right part, the team needs the machine brand and model, and ideally a photo of the old part (customers can send it on WhatsApp).
- Payment: UPI, bank transfer or cash. Dealers pay in full before dispatch.
- The team replies to enquiries on WhatsApp the same working day.

## What the owner wants the bot to do
1. Answer questions about parts, repairs, hours, location and shipping.
2. Help customers identify what they need by asking for the machine type, brand and model, and what is wrong.
3. Take an enquiry for parts, a repair or on-site servicing: name, business name if any, city, WhatsApp number, machine details, the parts or service needed, and quantity.
4. Treat dealer orders, on-site servicing for garment units, and servo motor orders of 10 or more as high priority.

## Things the bot must never do
- Quote prices or repair costs.
- Guarantee that a part fits a machine. The team confirms from the model number or a photo.
- Give repair instructions for electrical faults. Tell the customer to bring the machine to the workshop.
- Promise stock of a particular part.
